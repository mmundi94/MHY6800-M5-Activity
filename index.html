What else the team found

  • Pressly releases once a month. A finished "bulk upload" feature waited 12 days for the release.
  • A custom invoice layout built for one print shop has been used twice since release.
  • A bug was reopened after five weeks, and the developer needed a day to relearn the code.
  • Designers pass each item to developers, who pass it to testers. Each pass adds a wait before the next stage starts.
  • Two developers each have four items in progress at once.
  • Finished code waits about four days for the one senior developer who reviews it.
  • Six of the last 15 items failed testing and had to be reworked.

Three Pressly teams

  • Help desk: requests arrive at random, most fixes take under a day, and some can't wait.
  • Upload team: builds new, uncertain features toward a goal, and print-shop owners review its work every two weeks.
  • Template team: handles a steady stream of similar template updates, and waiting is its main problem.
